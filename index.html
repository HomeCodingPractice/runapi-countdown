How the lock screen works

The design

  • Neon terminal style: navy-black, mint green and violet
  • One huge number, with the sprint and next release below it

Staying on track

  • A daily phone reminder with that day's task
  • Weekly refresh of the number on the wallpaper
  • Sunday review of days left against the plan
